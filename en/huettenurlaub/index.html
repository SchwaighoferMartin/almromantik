Schmiedsipplalm · 1,300 m

Mountain hut holiday in Gosau

Our secluded self-catering hut for 1 to 6 guests. Wood-fired stove, barbecue area, starry skies.

The hut

High above Gosau, surrounded by meadows and forest, our hut stands on the Schmiedsipplalm. No neighbours, no traffic, and at night a starry sky you can hardly see in the valley any more.

You cook and heat with the wood-fired stove, and the chimney warms the sleeping room. Solar panels supply enough power for lights and the coffee machine. There's running water and a rustic shower, and the toilet is a classic outhouse. Real hut life.

Arrival

Pick up the key from us at Austraße 22 in Gosau, where you'll also get directions. In your own car you drive about 20 minutes along a forest road to the parking spot, then walk the last 300 m.

The hut can be booked from May to October. It is not accessible in winter.

Deliberately simple

What to expect at the hut

The hut is not a comfortable holiday home but genuine alpine living. Please read this before booking:

  • No fridge, but a cool pantry
  • Solar electricity, only for devices up to 500 watts, so no hairdryer
  • Cooking and heating are done with wood
  • Dry toilet instead of a flush toilet
  • Little mobile reception, mostly only above the hut
  • You walk the last 300 m and bring your own food

If that's exactly what you're looking for, you'll love the hut.

FAQ about the hut

When can the hut be booked?

From May to October. In winter the hut cannot be reached.

Is the hut really secluded?

Yes. A hiking trail passes below the hut, where the occasional walker goes by, but only a few. You have the peace and nature practically to yourself.

Where do I get the key?

At our home at Austraße 22 in Gosau. We'll explain everything important about the hut: the wood stove, water, electricity and the way up.

How do I get to the hut, what car do I need?

From our place you drive about 20 to 25 minutes uphill on a forest road. A normal car will get you to the two parking spaces; you walk the last 300 m. Keep that in mind when packing.

Are bed linen and towels included?

Yes, both are included. The bed linen is ready for you to put on yourself, and the towels are in the bathroom.

Is there electricity and hot water?

Electricity comes from solar panels. Charging phones, lights and our coffee machine work; devices over 500 watts such as hairdryers or vacuum cleaners don't. Hot water comes from the gas heater in the bathroom and from the water tank on the wood stove in the kitchen.

How is the hut heated, is there firewood?

Heating and cooking are done with the wood stove in the kitchen-living room, and the chimney also warms the bedroom. Firewood is included. We'll show you how to light the stove when we hand over the key.

Is there a fridge?

No. There's a cool pantry for your food.

Is there mobile reception?

Only a little, mostly on the A1 network. It works best just above the hut. In an emergency you can reach us on +43 664 11 44 926.

What about rubbish?

We dispose of it for you; this is included in the € 89 final cleaning fee, as are wood and gas.

From what age is the hut suitable for children?

We recommend it for children aged about 5 and up.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, by arrangement. Please let us know when you book.
