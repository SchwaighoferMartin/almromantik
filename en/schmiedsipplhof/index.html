Stone pine sauna with relaxation area and Kneipp pool

We built our sauna ourselves from Swiss stone pine. Next to it are the covered relaxation area with wooden benches and the Kneipp pool to cool off, and outside the terrace with deckchairs and a view of the Gosaukamm. Just switch it on and enjoy – free of charge for our guests.

Farm life

Our animals

5 suckler cows with their young, Bella the horse, the donkeys Emma and Marie, miniature Shetland ponies, Valais Blacknose sheep, runner ducks, cats and Sky the farm dog. For petting and feeding, come along with us – just let us know.

Cows

5 suckler cows with their young

We've been a suckler cow farm since 2015: our five Pinzgauer cows raise their calves themselves. In summer they spend their time on the alpine pasture, right near our mountain hut. Anyone staying there has them as neighbours.

Frequently asked questions

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Please register your pet when booking; it costs € 8 per animal per night. Pets are not allowed in the bedrooms or on the beds. If we can show that your pet has been there, we charge € 100 for extra cleaning. Please don't let it do its business in the meadows, as that's where we make hay for our animals.

Is the sauna included in the price?

Yes. Our guests can use the stone pine sauna and Kneipp pool free of charge.

Can we visit the animals?

You're welcome to watch and take photos at any time, but only from outside: please don't enter the stable or the pasture, as our cows protect their calves. For petting and feeding, just let us know and we'll go together – you'll get to know them all by name.

How far is the ski lift?

It's a 7 to 10-minute walk to the Hornspitz lift in the Dachstein West ski area. The cross-country trail starts right outside the farm door.

Can we buy products from the farm?

Yes. We make our own juices, fruit spreads and herbs. Just ask on arrival what's currently available.

Can we play in the meadows around the farm?

Please don't. We mow the meadows twice a year to make the hay our animals live on in winter. For play there is the playground, the garden and the lawn, and we're happy to show you around.

Is there a barbecue?

Yes, a barbecue is available for you. The bins are to the right of the house; please separate your rubbish, there's also an organic waste bin.

Do I get the SalzkammergutCard?

Yes. From 3 nights you get the SalzkammergutCard free of charge, with up to 25% off cable cars, caves, boat trips, museums and more. The forms are available at the farm.

What is added to the price?

The Gosau tourist tax and, where applicable, the final cleaning. You see the exact total price in the online booking before you confirm.
