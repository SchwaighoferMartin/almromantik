The Schwaighofer family

About us

Farm and mountain hut in the heart of the Salzkammergut.

We are Heike and Martin

We live in Gosau and welcome guests where we are at home. Since 2015 Martin has run the Schmiedsipplhof as a suckler cow farm, while Heike looks after the farm, the animals and the guests.

Martin is a master carpenter. He designed much of the furniture in our apartments himself, and we built the stone pine sauna on the farm with our own hands.
