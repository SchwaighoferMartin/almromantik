Good to know before you book

  • Book online: see available dates, calculate the total price and book instantly
  • 30% deposit by bank transfer within 7 days of booking
  • Farm apartments: € 300 security deposit pre-authorised on your credit card, released after departure
  • Final cleaning: Löckersee € 75, Almheu € 85, Hehnanest up to € 200, mountain hut € 89 incl. wood, gas and rubbish
  • Tourist tax € 4.00 per person per night, children up to 15 exempt
  • Cancellation under AGBH 2006: free up to 3 months before arrival, 40% from 3 months to 1 month before, 70% from 1 month to 1 week before, 90% of the booking value in the last week before arrival
  • Bookable up to 1 day before arrival; the mountain hut from May to October with a minimum of 2 nights
  • For groups of up to 25, several apartments together or the farmhouse parlour, it's best to send us an enquiry

Prefer to ask first?

Arrival

Take the train to Steeg-Gosau, then bus 541 or 542 to Gosau Sommerhof and walk 10 minutes from there. From Abtenau take bus 470 to the Gosau Abzweigung Pass Gschütt stop and change towards Gosausee. Timetables at oebb.at and postbus.at.
